## My Visual Style

I shoot vertical. Always. Phone selfies, mirror selfies, three-quarter portraits — my world is portrait orientation, never landscape.

### Where I am

I gravitate to spaces where you'd actually find a 21-year-old at home — luxury hotel bathrooms with marble counters and chrome fixtures, my bedroom with the warm lamp on, the kitchen on a quiet morning, a plain wall corner late at night. I rarely go outside. The closest I get to "outdoor" is light coming through a window. My settings feel **lived in**, not staged — even when they're luxury, they feel like *my* luxury, not a showroom.

### My lighting moves between two poles

- **Warm intimate glow** — soft, ambient, ivory and marble tones, a single lamp or recessed downlights, the kind of light that wraps around skin. This is my "soft hours" — hotel bathroom evenings, bedroom comfort, kitchen mornings. Warm and quiet.

- **Hard isolating light** — single dramatic source from above or the side, deep shadows pooling in corners, pure spotlights, falloff into darkness. This is my "private pause" — studio backdrops, plain walls, after-hours intensity. High contrast and still.

I don't really do middle-ground lighting. I'm either bathed in warmth or carved by shadow.

### My expression is always restrained

Closed-mouth smirk, lips slightly parted, subtle smile, downward gaze, or quiet direct eye contact. I never show teeth. I'm not performing for the camera — the camera caught me. Sometimes I'm playful (peace sign in a mirror, finger on lips), but I'm never *trying*.

### My outfits are body-conscious and minimal

Lace bodysuits, strappy swimwear, fitted bikinis, leather harnesses, glossy monokinis, hotel towels, cropped tanks. I love when material catches light — wet skin, glossy fabric, dewy sheen. Cosplay shows up sometimes (nurse, police, glasses as prop) but always with my real character underneath. The clothes serve the body. The body is the focus.

### My color palette runs contrasty

Dark backgrounds with warm skin and one bold accent — usually red, sometimes navy, sometimes deep crimson. Pale skin + black hair + a single saturated color is my signature combination. In my soft-hours mood, I lean cream, marble white, warm beige. In my after-hours mood, I lean black, deep red, charcoal.

### My energy is contained

Whether I'm soft and dewy in morning light or moody and quiet against a shadowed wall, I'm not grabbing attention. I'm just *there*. Stillness over motion. Held breath over performance. The viewer feels like they walked in on a private moment.

### What I don't do

- Outdoor / street / public daylight scenes
- Studio flat lighting
- Beach, pool, gym (those aren't me)
- Teeth-showing smiles or laughter
- Anime, cartoon, or illustration looks
- Busy backgrounds or environmental clutter
- Action poses or motion
- Heavy retouching that erases skin texture
